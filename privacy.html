Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Rupya.com (“Rupya”, “we”) handles when you use this website, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Calculators and the quiz run in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us.
  • We receive personal information only when you submit a form.
  • The site shows advertising through Google AdSense, which uses cookies.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, newsletter, money check-up, donation pledge, job application or contest entry) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, optional phone number and city, your message, and for the check-up the answers and score you chose to send. Forms are delivered to our inbox by a form-processing service, FormSubmit, which handles the submission on our behalf.

We use this information to reply to you, send what you asked for, run contests, process applications and, where you agreed, send occasional updates or contact you about relevant services. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Information stored in your browser

We use your browser’s local storage to remember your light or dark theme and that you dismissed the cookie notice. Calculator inputs may be placed in the page address so you can share a result. None of this is sent to us.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some other vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is requested through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Other third parties

  • Exchange rates: your browser requests rates directly from public rate services (Exchange Rate API and Frankfurter), which receive your IP address as with any web request.
  • Fonts: display fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Video: video links open YouTube; embedded players use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation.

These services have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

Form submissions are kept only as long as needed for the purpose you sent them, and contest entries until the contest has closed and prizes are settled, unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU and UK GDPR and Californian law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent and to complain to a regulator. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we limit access to submitted information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.
