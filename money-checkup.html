    How the score works

    The score adds four parts. Savings rate (35 points): the share of income you save or invest, with full marks at 30%. Emergency cover (25 points): months of expenses you could fund without income. Debt load (20 points): EMIs as a share of income, with full marks for none and zero above 50%. Protection (20 points): health and term life cover. It is a quick screen based on common planning rules of thumb, not a full financial plan or advice.
