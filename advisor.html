Questions

Is Rupya a financial adviser?

No. Rupya is an independent information service, not a bank, lender, insurer, broker or SEBI-registered investment adviser. Our replies explain your options and the questions to ask; for regulated advice we can point you to a registered professional.

Will my details be sold?

No. Your request is used to reply to you. It is shared with a partner only if you ask for an introduction, and only after we tell you who the partner is.

What if I only want to use the calculators?

That is fine. Every calculator works without sharing any details, and your inputs stay in your browser.
