Money glossary

The terms you meet in bank letters, fund factsheets and tax forms, each in one line.

AMC
Asset management company; the firm that runs a mutual fund.
Amortisation
The schedule showing how each EMI splits between interest and principal.
Annuity
A product that converts a lump sum into regular income for a period or for life.
Asset allocation
How your money is divided between equity, debt, gold and cash.
Basis point
One hundredth of a percentage point; 25 basis points is 0.25%.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate; the steady yearly rate linking a start and end value.
Capital gain
Profit from selling an asset for more than you paid.
Cess
A tax on tax; the health and education cess adds 4% to income tax.
Credit utilisation
The share of your credit card limit that you are using.
Debt fund
A mutual fund that invests in bonds and money-market instruments.
Demat account
An account that holds shares and other securities in electronic form.
DICGC
The RBI subsidiary that insures bank deposits up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank.
Direct plan
The version of a mutual fund bought without a distributor, with a lower expense ratio.
ELSS
Equity-linked savings scheme; an equity fund with a three-year lock-in and an old-regime tax deduction.
EMI
Equated monthly instalment; the fixed monthly loan repayment.
EPF
Employees’ Provident Fund; the retirement account funded by employee and employer.
Expense ratio
The yearly fee a fund charges, as a percentage of your investment.
Exit load
A fee charged if you redeem fund units before a set period.
Floating rate
An interest rate that changes with a benchmark such as the repo rate.
GST
Goods and services tax; India’s tax on the supply of goods and services.
HRA
House rent allowance; a salary component partly exempt for renters under the old regime.
Index fund
A fund that copies a market index rather than picking stocks.
Inflation
The rate at which prices rise and money loses buying power.
KYC
Know your customer; the identity check required before investing or opening accounts.
Liquid fund
A debt fund holding very short-term instruments, used for parking cash.
LTCG
Long-term capital gain; profit on an asset held beyond its qualifying period.
Mid-market rate
The midpoint between buying and selling prices of a currency.
NAV
Net asset value; the price of one unit of a mutual fund.
NPS
National Pension System; a market-linked retirement scheme regulated by PFRDA.
NRE / NRO
Rupee accounts for non-residents: NRE for foreign earnings, NRO for Indian income.
PPF
Public Provident Fund; a 15-year government savings scheme with tax-free interest.
Principal
The amount borrowed or invested, before interest or returns.
Rebate
A reduction in tax payable for incomes below a set limit.
Repo rate
The rate at which the RBI lends to banks; it steers loan and deposit rates.
SIP
Systematic investment plan; a fixed monthly investment into a fund.
Standard deduction
A flat amount salaried people subtract from income without proof.
STCG
Short-term capital gain; profit on an asset sold within its qualifying period.
Surcharge
Extra tax on the tax of people with income above ₹50 lakh.
SWP
Systematic withdrawal plan; a fixed regular withdrawal from a fund.
TDS
Tax deducted at source by the payer, such as an employer or bank.
Term insurance
Life cover that pays only on death during the policy term, with no maturity value.
UPI
Unified Payments Interface; the instant bank-to-bank payment system run by NPCI.
XIRR
The annualised return on a series of cash flows at irregular dates, used for SIPs.
