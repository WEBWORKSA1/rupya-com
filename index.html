Common questions

What is Rupya.com?

Rupya is a free, independent money toolkit for India. “Rupya” is simply the everyday Hindi word for rupee. The site offers calculators, a live rupee converter and educational guides. It does not sell financial products and is not a bank, broker or adviser.

Are the calculators accurate?

They use the standard formulas shown on each page and current published rules, such as the FY 2026-27 income tax slabs. Results are estimates: actual returns, interest rates, charges and taxes can differ, so confirm important figures with your bank, fund house or a qualified professional.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Rates are mid-market reference rates refreshed daily from public data sources. The rate you get from a bank, card or money-transfer service will include their margin and fees.

Is my data stored?

Calculator inputs stay in your browser. We only receive information you choose to send through a form. See the privacy policy.
