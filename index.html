Common questions

What is Rupya.com?

A free, independent money toolkit for India and Indians abroad. “Rupya” is simply the everyday Hindi word for the rupee. Rupya is not a bank, lender, broker or adviser, and it does not sell financial products.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use the standard formulas printed on each page and the current published rules, such as the FY 2026-27 income tax slabs and the October to December 2026 small savings rates. Results are estimates: confirm important numbers with your bank, fund house or a qualified professional.

Where do the live rates come from?

Currency rates are mid-market reference rates from public data services, refreshed through the day. Gold and silver prices start from the international spot price and add India’s 15% import duty. Every rate page shows its source and update time.

Is my data stored?

Numbers you type into calculators stay in your browser. Saved results live only on your device. We receive information only when you choose to send a form. See the privacy policy.
