Japanese yen to Indian rupee

One yen is worth less than a rupee, so prices in Japan look large. Convert them here for travel, study or business.

Reading yen prices

Because the yen’s unit value is small, banks in India quote it per 100 yen. The quick conversion table above shows common amounts. A useful habit when travelling is to remember what ¥1,000 is in rupees and scale from there.

What moves the yen

For decades the Bank of Japan kept interest rates near zero, which encouraged investors to borrow in yen and invest elsewhere. That weakened the yen and made it sensitive to global risk: in market panics, those trades unwind and the yen often jumps. Changes in Japanese interest rates now move it sharply.

Questions

Is Japan cash-heavy?

Less than it used to be, but some smaller shops and rural places still prefer cash. Carry some yen alongside a forex card.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Yen cash in India can carry a wider margin than cards; compare before you buy.
