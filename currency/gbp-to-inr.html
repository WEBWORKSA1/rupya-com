British pound to Indian rupee

For the large Indian community in Britain, students paying UK fees and anyone moving money between the two countries.

What drives the pound against the rupee

Like most rupee rates outside the dollar, GBP/INR is a cross rate: the pound in dollars times the dollar in rupees. Bank of England rate decisions, UK inflation data and British politics move sterling; oil prices and foreign investment flows move the rupee. The pound is one of the highest-valued major currencies, so small percentage moves mean large rupee amounts on big transfers.

Questions

What is the cheapest way to send pounds to India?

Online remittance services and UK banking apps often beat high-street bank transfers. Compare the rupees delivered after the fee and margin, not the headline rate. Money sent to an NRE account can be repatriated freely later.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Providers add a spread; the best online services stay close to it.

Do I pay tax in India on money sent from the UK?

Transfers of your own income or savings are not taxed again just for being sent. Interest earned on NRE deposits in India is tax-free in India; NRO interest is taxable. Your UK tax position depends on your residence.
