Bahraini dinar to Indian rupee

The second most valuable currency unit after the Kuwaiti dinar. Convert Bahraini dinars to rupees at today’s mid-market rate.

Fixed at 0.376 to the dollar

Bahrain pegs its dinar at 0.376 to the US dollar, which makes one dinar worth about 2.66 dollars. The rate to the rupee is therefore the dollar–rupee rate multiplied by about 2.66, and follows it exactly. Like the Kuwaiti dinar, it is divided into 1,000 fils.

Questions

Can I use Bahraini dinars in Saudi Arabia?

Some shops near the causeway accept them informally, but they are not legal tender there. Exchange them for riyals for anything substantial.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Remittance providers add a margin and sometimes a fee.
