Australian dollar to Indian rupee

Australia is a leading destination for Indian students and migrants. Convert Australian dollars to rupees and see the trend.

A commodity currency

Australia earns much of its export income from iron ore, coal, gas and farm goods, so the Australian dollar tends to rise when commodity prices and global growth are strong and fall when investors grow cautious. It is also sensitive to China’s economy, its largest customer, and to Reserve Bank of Australia rate decisions.

Questions

How should students budget for currency swings?

Build a margin of 5% to 10% into your budget, and for large fee payments consider converting in parts. A rupee that weakens by a few percent over a two-year course can add a lot to the cost.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Education remittances through banks and specialist services carry a margin; compare total cost.
