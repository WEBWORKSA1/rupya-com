Kuwaiti dinar to Indian rupee

One Kuwaiti dinar buys more rupees than any other currency unit in the world. Convert it at today’s rate.

Why the dinar is worth so much

A currency’s unit value says nothing about the strength of its economy; it reflects how the unit was first set. Kuwait set a high value when it introduced the dinar and has kept it there with large oil reserves behind it. Unlike its Gulf neighbours, Kuwait does not peg to the dollar alone. Since 2007 it has managed the dinar against an undisclosed basket of currencies, which is dominated by the dollar, so KWD/INR mostly follows USD/INR with small differences.

The dinar is divided into 1,000 fils, so rates are often quoted to three decimal places.

Questions

Is a high-value currency a better currency?

No. What matters for your savings is how its value changes over time, not the size of one unit.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Exchange companies in Kuwait add a margin; compare the rupees delivered.
