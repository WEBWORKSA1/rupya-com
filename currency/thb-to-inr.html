Thai baht to Indian rupee

Thailand is one of the most visited foreign destinations for Indian travellers. Know what a price in baht means in rupees before you pay.

Spending baht wisely

  • Thai ATMs charge a fixed fee per foreign card withdrawal, on top of your bank’s charges, so withdraw larger amounts less often.
  • When a card machine or ATM offers to charge you in rupees, decline and pay in baht. The conversion it offers is usually worse.
  • Money changers in tourist areas often give better rates for cash than airport counters.

What moves the baht

Tourism and exports such as cars, electronics and rice drive Thailand’s economy, so the baht strengthens when visitor numbers and global demand are strong.

Questions

Can I pay with UPI in Thailand?

UPI acceptance abroad is expanding through partnerships with local payment networks, but coverage is patchy. Carry a card and some cash as backup.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Cash exchange and cards add a margin.
