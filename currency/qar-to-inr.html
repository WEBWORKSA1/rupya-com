Qatari riyal to Indian rupee

Indians form the largest expatriate community in Qatar. Convert riyals to rupees at today’s mid-market rate.

Pegged at 3.64

The Qatar Central Bank has fixed the riyal at 3.64 to the US dollar since 2001. That makes the Qatari riyal worth slightly more than the Saudi riyal, which is fixed at 3.75, and means QAR/INR rises and falls exactly in step with USD/INR.

Questions

Why is the Qatari riyal worth more than the Saudi riyal?

Fewer Qatari riyals buy a dollar: 3.64 against 3.75. So one Qatari riyal buys about 3% more rupees than one Saudi riyal.

Is it better to send money at month end?

Demand for remittances peaks just after payday, but rates are set by global markets, not by Qatar’s pay cycle. Compare providers rather than dates.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Exchange houses and banks add a margin.
