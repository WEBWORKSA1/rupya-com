New Zealand dollar to Indian rupee

For students, skilled migrants and travellers between New Zealand and India. Convert at today’s mid-market rate.

What moves the kiwi

New Zealand’s exports are dominated by dairy, meat and other farm produce, so global dairy prices and demand from China and Australia move its currency. The Reserve Bank of New Zealand’s interest rate decisions matter too. Thinly traded compared with the big currencies, the kiwi can swing more on global risk sentiment.

Questions

Why does NZD often move with AUD?

The two economies are closely linked by trade and both depend on commodities and Asian demand, so their currencies tend to rise and fall together.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Banks and transfer services add a margin.
