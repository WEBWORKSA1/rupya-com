Hong Kong dollar to Indian rupee

Hong Kong is a major hub for India’s diamond, jewellery and electronics trade. Convert Hong Kong dollars to rupees here.

The linked exchange rate

Since 1983 Hong Kong has kept its dollar between 7.75 and 7.85 to the US dollar, defending the band with its large reserves. So HKD/INR follows USD/INR closely, with only small moves within the band.

Questions

Why are there three kinds of Hong Kong banknotes?

Three commercial banks are authorised to issue notes, backed by US dollars held with the government. All are equally valid, alongside government-issued notes and coins.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Providers add a margin.
