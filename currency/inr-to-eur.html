Indian rupee to euro

Budgeting for a Schengen holiday or a semester in Germany, France or Ireland? Convert rupees to euros here.

Budgeting in euros

Build your trip or study budget in euros first, then convert, and add 3% to 5% for exchange margins and rate moves. Students who must show funds in a blocked account before a visa should check the required amount for the year they apply, since it is revised periodically.

Questions

Is a forex card better than an international debit card?

A forex card locks your rate at loading and usually has lower markups. A debit card converts at the rate on each transaction plus a markup and GST. For long stays, a local bank account is cheapest.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Banks sell euros at a higher rupee price.
