Omani rial to Indian rupee

Oman and India have centuries of trade across the Arabian Sea, and a large Indian community there today. Convert rials to rupees here.

A high-value rial tied to the dollar

The Central Bank of Oman has fixed the rial at 0.3845 to the US dollar since 1986, so one rial is worth about 2.60 dollars. The rial–rupee rate is the dollar–rupee rate multiplied by about 2.6, and moves in step with it. The rial is divided into 1,000 baisa.

Questions

Is the Omani rial the same as the Saudi or Qatari riyal?

No. They share a name but are different currencies with different values. One Omani rial is worth roughly ten Saudi riyals.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Banks and exchange houses add a margin; compare what arrives in rupees.
