Swiss franc to Indian rupee

The currency investors run to when markets panic. Convert Swiss francs to rupees for a trip to the Alps or a business payment.

A safe-haven currency

Switzerland’s political stability, low inflation and large current account surplus make the franc a refuge in times of stress, so it often strengthens when global markets fall. The Swiss National Bank sometimes sells francs to stop it rising too far, because a very strong franc hurts Swiss exporters. Over the long run the franc has gained against most currencies, including the rupee.

Questions

Can I use euros in Switzerland?

Some shops and stations accept euros, usually at a poor rate, with change given in francs. Paying in francs by card is normally cheaper.

Is this the rate I will get?

It is the mid-market rate. Banks and forex cards add a margin.
