Writing a cheque safely

  • Write the amount in words right after “Rupees” and end with “Only” so nothing can be added.
  • Draw a line through any empty space after the words and the figures.
  • Make sure the words and the figures match; banks can refuse a cheque where they differ.
  • Cross the cheque with two parallel lines if it should only be paid into a bank account.
How do I write paise in words?

After the rupees, add “and” followed by the paise in words, then “Only”. For example, ₹1,250.50 is “Rupees One Thousand Two Hundred and Fifty and Fifty Paise Only”.

Should I write “lakh” or “lakhs”?

Either is understood, but the singular “lakh” and “crore” are standard after a number: “Rupees Twelve Lakh Fifty Thousand Only”.
