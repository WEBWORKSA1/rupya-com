How to judge a finance video

Anyone can publish money advice. Before acting on a video, check four things. Who is speaking: regulators such as SEBI and the RBI, and registered advisers, are accountable for what they say. What they earn: a presenter paid to promote a product should say so. Whether returns are promised: guaranteed high returns do not exist, and urgency is a warning sign. Whether the rules are current: tax slabs and scheme rates change, so compare what you hear with our dated guides.
