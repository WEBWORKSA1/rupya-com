Reading an IFSC

The Indian Financial System Code identifies a bank branch for electronic payments. The first four letters are the bank, the fifth character is always zero, and the last six identify the branch. You will find it on your cheque book, passbook and in your banking app.

Questions

What is a MICR code?

A nine-digit code printed on cheques that identifies the bank and branch for cheque clearing. The first three digits are the city, the next three the bank and the last three the branch.

Does the IFSC change if a bank merges?

It can. After a merger, branches of the merged bank usually receive new codes after a transition period. Always use the code your bank currently shows.

Is it safe to share my IFSC?

Yes. An IFSC identifies a branch, not your account. To receive money you share your account number, name and IFSC; never share PINs, passwords or OTPs.
