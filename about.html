About Rupya

Rupya is a free money toolkit for India. The name is the everyday Hindi word for rupee, and the idea is as plain as the name: every rupee, worked out.

What we do

Most money decisions come down to a number. What will this SIP be worth? What does this loan really cost? Which tax regime leaves more in hand? What is a dollar worth in rupees today? Rupya answers those questions with tools that show their working, and with short guides that explain the rules behind them.

  • Calculators built on standard formulas and current Indian rules, each with a chart, a year-by-year table and a shareable link.
  • Live rupee rates against 25 currencies, with a 90-day chart and an amount-in-words tool.
  • Guides and a glossary written in plain English and dated when updated.
  • A money check-up and quiz that turn learning into next steps.

What we do not do

Rupya is not a bank, broker, insurer, lender or registered investment adviser. We do not sell financial products, hold money or give personal advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

How we keep things accurate

Figures such as tax slabs and scheme rates come from published government and regulator notices. Each guide shows the month it was last updated. Calculator pages state their formulas and assumptions so you can check them. When we get something wrong, we correct it; please tell us.
