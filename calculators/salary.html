Salary calculator: CTC to in-hand

Your offer letter shows CTC. Your bank account sees something smaller. Here is the gap, line by line.

From CTC to take-home

CTC includes money you never receive monthly: the employer’s PF contribution and gratuity. From what remains, your own PF, professional tax and income tax are deducted. On a ₹12 lakh CTC with basic at half, PF on the ₹15,000 ceiling and gratuity included, monthly take-home under the new regime is about ₹93,800. Income tax is nil because taxable income stays under the ₹12 lakh rebate limit after the ₹75,000 standard deduction.

A rebate makes tax nil up to ₹12 lakh of taxable income, with marginal relief just above it. A 4% cess applies on tax. For a full comparison with the old regime, use the income tax calculator.

Why is my PF deduction only ₹1,800?

Many employers calculate PF on the statutory wage ceiling of ₹15,000 a month: 12% of ₹15,000 is ₹1,800. Others deduct 12% of full basic, which lowers take-home but builds a bigger EPF corpus.

Is professional tax the same everywhere?

No. It is a state tax, up to ₹2,500 a year, and some states do not levy it. Set it to zero if yours does not.
