Personal loan EMI calculator

Personal loans are quick and unsecured, which makes them expensive. See exactly how expensive before you accept an offer.

The full cost

₹5 lakh at 12% for three years costs about ₹16,607 a month and about ₹97,858 in interest. Add a 2% processing fee and GST on it, and the cost passes ₹1.07 lakh. Always compare the annual percentage rate including fees, which lenders must show in a key fact statement before you sign.

Before you borrow

  • Use the loan for needs, not wants; personal loan rates are among the highest in the market.
  • Instant app loans can carry very high effective rates. Borrow only from banks, NBFCs or apps linked to an RBI-regulated lender.
  • Never pay an “upfront fee” to receive a loan; that is a common fraud.
Can I prepay a personal loan?

Usually yes, after a minimum number of EMIs, often with a foreclosure charge. Check the sanction letter.

What credit score do lenders want?

Most look for 750 or more for the best rates. See our guide to credit scores.
