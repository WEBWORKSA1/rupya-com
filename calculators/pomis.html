Post Office MIS calculator

The Monthly Income Scheme turns a lump sum into a fixed monthly payment for five years, with government backing.

How the scheme works

Deposit up to ₹9 lakh in a single account or ₹15 lakh in a joint account. Interest at 7.4% a year is paid every month into your post office savings account. After five years the deposit is returned. ₹9 lakh pays ₹5,550 a month.

Monthly income = Deposit × rate ÷ 12

Questions

Is the income taxable?

Yes, at your slab rate. There is no Section 80C benefit on the deposit.

Can I reinvest the monthly income?

Yes. A common approach is to route it into a post office recurring deposit, which compounds the income.

Can I withdraw early?

After one year, with a deduction of 2% of the deposit before three years and 1% after.
