Discount calculator

“30% off plus 10% extra” is not 40% off. See the real price and the real discount.

Stacked discounts multiply

The second discount applies to the already reduced price. ₹2,999 with 30% off becomes ₹2,099; a further 10% takes it to about ₹1,889. The combined discount is 37%, not 40%.

Final price = Price × (1 − d1) × (1 − d2)

Before you check out

  • Bank offers often have a cap, such as 10% up to ₹1,500, and a minimum spend.
  • Compare the final price with what the item sold for before the sale; inflated “original” prices are common.
  • No-cost EMI offers may carry a processing fee and GST on interest; check the total.
