Car loan EMI calculator

What the car really costs once interest and fees are counted, and how the tenure changes it.

Reading the result

An ₹8 lakh loan at 9.25% for five years costs about ₹16,704 a month, with about ₹2 lakh in interest and an ₹8,000 processing fee. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but keeps you paying interest on a car that loses value every year.

Getting a better deal

  • Ask whether the rate is a reducing-balance rate. Some dealer offers quote a flat rate, which looks lower but costs more. Check with the flat vs reducing calculator.
  • A larger down payment lowers both the EMI and the interest.
  • Compare the lender’s foreclosure charges if you may repay early.
How long should a car loan be?

Three to five years for most buyers. Beyond that, the loan often outlasts the car’s best years.

Is car loan interest tax-deductible?

Not for a personal car. A car used for business may allow a deduction against business income; ask a tax professional.
