EPF calculator

Your provident fund grows quietly every month. See what it will be worth when you retire.

Where the money comes from

You contribute 12% of basic plus DA. Your employer also contributes 12%, but up to 8.33% of wages capped at ₹15,000, a maximum of ₹1,250 a month, goes to the Employees’ Pension Scheme; the rest goes to your EPF. Interest, 8.25% for FY 2025-26, is calculated monthly and credited once a year.

A ₹30,000 basic at age 28, rising 6% a year, builds about ₹1.84 crore by 58 at 8.25%.

Questions

Is EPF interest tax-free?

Yes, on your own contributions up to ₹2.5 lakh a year (₹5 lakh where the employer does not contribute). Interest on contributions above that is taxable.

Can I withdraw before retirement?

Partial withdrawals are allowed for specific needs such as a home, medical treatment, education or marriage, subject to service conditions. Withdrawals within five years of service are taxable.

Should I move to VPF?

The Voluntary Provident Fund earns the same rate and safety. It suits investors who want a debt allocation, within the tax-free contribution limit.
