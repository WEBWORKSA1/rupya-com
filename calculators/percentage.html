Why does a 10% fall followed by a 10% rise leave me lower?

Each percentage applies to a different base. ₹100 falls 10% to ₹90; 10% of ₹90 is ₹9, so it only rises back to ₹99.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

A rate rising from 5% to 6% is a rise of one percentage point, but a 20% increase in the rate itself.
