FIRE calculator

Financial independence means your investments can pay your expenses for life. Find the number, and the age you reach it.

Your FIRE number

FIRE number = Yearly expenses ÷ Safe withdrawal rate

At ₹60,000 a month and a 3.5% withdrawal rate, you need about ₹2.06 crore in today’s money. The calculator grows that target with inflation and grows your investments with your returns until the two meet. With ₹10 lakh invested and ₹40,000 added monthly at 11%, that happens at about 57.

Why 3.5% and not 4%

The well-known 4% rule comes from US data with lower inflation. Indian inflation has run higher, and an early retirement may last 40 years or more, so many planners use 3% to 3.5%.

Getting there sooner

  • Spending less helps twice: you save more and need a smaller number.
  • Raise your investment every year with your income.
  • Keep health insurance separate; one hospital stay can undo years of saving.
