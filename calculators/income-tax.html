What the calculator applies

  • Standard deduction for salaried people and pensioners: ₹75,000 in the new regime, ₹50,000 in the old.
  • Rebate: no tax in the new regime when taxable income is ₹12 lakh or less (so a salary up to ₹12.75 lakh), with marginal relief just above that level; no tax in the old regime up to ₹5 lakh.
  • Surcharge above ₹50 lakh and 4% health and education cess on the total.

Income taxed at special rates, such as capital gains, is outside this calculator. Read new vs old tax regime for how to choose.

Questions

Which regime applies by default?

The new regime is the default. Salaried taxpayers can choose the old regime each year when filing, provided the return is filed on time.

At what deduction level does the old regime win?

It depends on income. As a rough guide, at ₹15 lakh of salary you need well over ₹5 lakh of deductions and exemptions before the old regime becomes cheaper. Move the deduction slider above to find your own break-even.
