Net worth calculator

One number that tells you whether you are moving forward. Work it out once, then check it every six months.

What to include

Use today’s values: bank balances, FDs, the current value of shares and funds, EPF, PPF and NPS balances, gold at today’s rate and a realistic property value. Subtract every loan outstanding and card due. The example adds ₹16 lakh of assets and subtracts ₹50,000 of debt for a net worth of ₹15.5 lakh.

Your result stays in your browser. Press Save to keep it on this device and compare next time; nothing is sent to us.

Reading the mix

  • If most of your wealth is one home, you may be asset-rich and cash-poor.
  • If almost nothing is in growth assets such as equity, inflation is eroding it.
  • A falling net worth with a rising income usually means lifestyle debt.
