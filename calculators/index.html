How to use these calculators well

A calculator is only as good as its assumptions. Three habits make the numbers more useful. First, run a cautious case alongside your expected case: if you assume 12% a year for equity, also look at 9%. Second, think in today’s rupees for long goals by pairing any result with the inflation calculator. Third, remember that tax and charges sit outside most formulas; a fund’s expense ratio, a loan’s processing fee or tax on FD interest all reduce what you keep.
