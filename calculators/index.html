Getting useful answers from a calculator

A calculator is only as good as its assumptions. Run a cautious case beside your expected one: if you assume 12% a year for equity, look at 9% too. Think in today’s rupees for long goals by pairing any result with the inflation calculator. And remember what sits outside most formulas: fund expense ratios, loan processing fees and tax on interest all reduce what you keep.

Every calculator has a Compare tab. Pin your current inputs as scenario A, change one number, and see the difference line by line. Use Save to keep a result in My Rupya on this device.
