Cost of delay calculator

“I’ll start next year” is the most expensive sentence in personal finance. Here is what it costs in rupees.

The arithmetic of waiting

Take ₹10,000 a month for 25 years at 12%. Starting today grows to about ₹1.9 crore. Starting five years later, with the same monthly amount and the same end date, gives about ₹1 crore. The five missing years cost roughly ₹90 lakh, even though only ₹6 lakh of contributions was skipped, because the earliest money compounds for longest.

To reach the same ₹1.9 crore after a five-year delay, you would need about ₹19,000 a month instead of ₹10,000.

Questions

Should I wait for the market to fall before starting?

Nobody times the market reliably, and waiting has a known cost shown above. A SIP spreads purchases over time, which reduces the risk of starting at a peak.

What if I can only invest a little now?

Start with what you can and raise it every year. A small SIP started today usually beats a large one started years later. Try the step-up SIP calculator.
