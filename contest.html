Creator challenge

Make a video of 60 seconds or less that explains one money idea for Indian viewers, using any Rupya calculator or guide. The best entries are featured on Rupya’s video page each month, and creators are considered for paid work. When a prize pool is funded, it is announced here before entries close.

Contest rules

  1. Entry is free. No purchase or donation is needed, and neither improves your chances.
  2. Open to people aged 18 or older. One quiz entry per person per calendar month.
  3. To qualify for the quiz draw, score at least 7 out of 10 and submit the entry form in the same month.
  4. Prizes are offered only in months when a prize pool has been funded and announced on this page. In other months the quiz runs for learning only.
  5. Winners are drawn at random from qualifying entries after the month ends and are contacted by email. A winner who does not reply within 14 days forfeits the prize and another entry is drawn.
  6. Prizes are as announced, are not transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Winners are responsible for any tax on prizes.
  7. Entries made with false details or automated tools are disqualified.
  8. The contest is void where prohibited by law. Rupya.com may change or cancel a contest that cannot run as planned.
  9. Entry details are used only to run the contest, as described in the privacy policy.
