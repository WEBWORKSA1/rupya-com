Prizes

The prize pool for each month is funded by supporters and sponsors and is announced on this page before the draw. If no prize pool has been announced for a month, no draw is held and the quiz runs for learning only. Want to fund a prize? Support Rupya or sponsor the contest.

Contest rules

  1. Entry is free. No purchase or donation is needed, and neither improves your chances.
  2. Open to people aged 18 or older. One entry per person per calendar month.
  3. To qualify, score at least 7 out of 10 and submit the entry form in the same month.
  4. Winners are chosen by random draw from qualifying entries after the month ends and are contacted by email. A winner who does not reply within 14 days forfeits the prize and another is drawn.
  5. Prizes are as announced on this page, are not transferable and carry no cash alternative unless stated. Winners are responsible for any tax on prizes.
  6. Entries made with false details or automated tools are disqualified.
  7. The contest is void where prohibited by law. Rupya.com may change or cancel a contest if it cannot run as planned.
  8. Entry details are used only to run the contest, as described in the privacy policy.
