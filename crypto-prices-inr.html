Know the risks. Cryptocurrencies are not legal tender in India and are not backed by the government or the RBI. Prices can fall sharply in hours. Rupya shows prices for information only and does not recommend buying or selling any coin.

How crypto is taxed in India

  • Profit on selling or swapping crypto is taxed at a flat 30%, plus 4% cess, whatever your income slab.
  • Only the purchase cost can be deducted. Losses on crypto cannot be set off against other income or carried forward.
  • A 1% tax is deducted at source on transfers above set annual limits.
  • Crypto received as a gift can be taxable in the receiver’s hands.

Questions

Why is the INR price different from what my exchange shows?

Indian exchanges have their own order books, and their price can sit above or below the global price converted to rupees. The prices here are global averages.

Is Tether really worth one dollar?

Tether and USD Coin are stablecoins that aim to hold a value of one US dollar. Their rupee price therefore moves mostly with the dollar-rupee rate.
